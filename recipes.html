    Spicy Chicken Burger

    Spicy Chicken Burger

    Ingredients:

  • Chicken: 1 lb ground chicken or 4 boneless chicken thighs.
  • Spice Mix: 1 tsp each of paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt.
  • Heat: 1 tsp cayenne pepper or chili powder (adjust to your preference).
  • 1 egg and 1/3 cup breadcrumbs.

    • Instructions

    1. Prep Patty: In a bowl, combine chicken with the spice mix, heat, egg, and breadcrumbs. Mix gently and form into 4 patties.
    2. Cook: Heat a skillet.Cook patties for 5–6 minutes per side until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
    Assemble: Spread spicy mayo on toasted buns. Layer with lettuce, tomato, and the hot chicken patty.
